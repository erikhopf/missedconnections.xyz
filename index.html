About the Show

Sketchy & Convoluted
Throughlines

Algorithms have failed us. Spotify only plays stuff it already knows you like, because it doesn't want you to shut it off. Our goal isn't making gobs of money — we're not trying to make any money — it's playing good music.

Listen along as a former record store employee and an ex-college radio DJ make sketchy and convoluted throughlines to connect music that shouldn't work, but actually kind of does. Expect deep cuts, B-sides, and the occasional track that'll make you wonder if we've lost our minds... until it clicks and you realize it's exactly what you needed to hear.

If you're in Portland, catch us on the dial at 90.3 FM or 98.3 FM, or anywhere in the world at freeformportland.org — every other Saturday from 8 to 10 PM.
